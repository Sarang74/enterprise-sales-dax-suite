## 🎯 Executive Summary & Business Problem

Leadership lacked visibility into true net profitability due to complex multi-tier discount structures, currency fluctuations across global regions, and slow, monolithic legacy reports that took upwards of 45 seconds to render.

- **The Objective:** Architect an automated, end-to-end analytical data mart and an executive-ready Power BI semantic model capable of delivering real-time gross margin decomposition with sub-second visual rendering across 5M+ transactional records.
- **Key Business Impacts:**
  - Identified **3.8% discount margin leakage** across low-performing customer tiers.
  - Reduced report refresh latency from **45 seconds to <800ms** via DAX optimization and star schema restructuring.
  - Consolidated 6 fragmented regional spreadsheets into a single enterprise source of truth.

---

## 📐 Data Architecture & Dimensional Model

The semantic model follows a strict **Kimball Star Schema** design. Bidirectional relationships and circular dependencies were completely eliminated to maximize VertiPaq engine compression.
